Real Estate Roundtable & Networking Mixer

This session will allow attendees to meet with real estate professionals across all different industries in a small and intimate setting. Each SME will be assigned a small 10 person round table in which attendees can approach the SME at the tables and casually ask questions that are general or specific in nature related to their respective fields. The conversation is causal and light. Other attendees can join the table and listen in or ask their own questions. Once they are done (at their pace), they can move to another table with a different type of SME. The mixer session will allow attendees to meet real estate professionals across all different segments, markets and professions. This will be a networking event to share ideas, knowledge, insights, deal flow and just casually ponder topics that weren't addressed in the previous sessions.
