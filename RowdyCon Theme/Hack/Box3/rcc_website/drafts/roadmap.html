Unshipped homepage concepts

These are the layouts the web team kicked around before locking the retro browser shell for RowdyCyberCon 2026.

Draft 01

Dial-up Hero

  • Headline: "San Antonio's student cyber meetup."
  • Primary CTA pushes registration above the fold.
  • Status bar animates fake modem connect sounds.
  • Rejected because the sound gag got old fast.
Draft 02

Workshop Launcher

  • Hero focused on hands-on labs and challenge rooms.
  • Secondary card advertised the partner packet.
  • Address bar rotated to /register and /sign-in.
  • Felt too corporate for a student-first event.
Draft 03

Bug Report Edition

  • Top nav highlighted the Report a Bug link.
  • FAQ opened in floating windows over the desktop.
  • April 11 date badge flashed like a taskbar alert.
  • Rejected after accessibility review.
Draft 04

CTF Cabinet

  • Used stacked mini windows for CTF, talks, and partners.
  • Leaned harder into the retro desktop motif.
  • One hidden link accidentally pointed to /admin-console.
  • That link is gone. The folder is not.
